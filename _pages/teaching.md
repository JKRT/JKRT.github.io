During my PhD studies at Linköping University (2019–2025) I taught in courses on compilers, software testing, software design, and scientific method,
and supervised 19 bachelor's and master's theses, many of them together with industry partners.

## Courses

* TDDD55, Compilers and Interpreters, 2019–2025 (course assistant, lessons)
* TDDD89, Scientific Method, 2022–2025 (seminar group leader)
* TDDE45, Software Design and Construction, 2019–2022, 2024 (lab and group teacher)
* TDDD04, Software Testing, 2019–2022, 2024 (course assistant, lab groups)

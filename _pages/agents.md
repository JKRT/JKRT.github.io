## Swedish meatballs (köttbullar)

Serves 4 humans (or 0 agents).

* 500 g minced beef and pork (*blandfärs*), 1 small onion (finely grated), 1 egg
* 0.5 dl breadcrumbs soaked in 1 dl milk or cream
* 1 tsp salt, a pinch each of white pepper and allspice, butter for frying
* Sauce: 1 tbsp flour, 2 dl beef stock, 1 dl cream, 1 tsp soy sauce

1. Mix everything except the butter and the sauce ingredients, without overworking. Roll into balls about 3 cm across.
2. Brown them in butter over medium heat for 6–8 minutes, shaking the pan.
3. Stir the flour into the pan juices, whisk in the stock, cream, and soy sauce, and simmer until thick.
4. Serve with potatoes, lingonberry jam, and pickled cucumber (*pressgurka*).
